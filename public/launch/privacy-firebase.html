MANTRA Firebase privacy policy

About this edition

MANTRA Firebase is a separate, authorized-account edition. Effective 4 November 2026. Contact: satwik1330@gmail.com. Its data practices differ from the offline Local edition.

Local records and optional cloud connection

The app stores diary records locally. When the authorized account connects, Firebase Authentication processes sign-in credentials and identifiers, and Firestore synchronizes diary records including daily notes, goals, tasks, events, amounts, saved links, completion history and daily screen-time totals. Detailed per-app usage is kept on the phone. The existing backend is restricted to the authorized account; public sign-up is unavailable.

Installation analytics

This edition reports an anonymous installation identifier, edition/version, first/last report dates and counts of configured goals, events, notifications, alarms, expenses and notes to Firebase. Analytics do not include note text, titles, financial amounts, saved links or detailed app usage. Firebase and network providers may process service/connection metadata.

Permissions and controls

Usage access is optional for screen-time review. Notification, exact-alarm and full-screen access support user-scheduled reminders and alarms; the ringing service shows controls and stops on Stop or timeout. You can revoke permissions in Android settings.

Retention and deletion

Signing out stops account sync but does not delete existing cloud records. Deleting a record may retain a deletion marker for synchronization. Clearing app data removes device copies, not Firestore or analytics records. The authorized account owner may request removal of cloud records and analytics at satwik1330@gmail.com. Local data is not backed up by Android.

Providers and contact

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are Google services. Their privacy practices apply to their processing. External saved links open other apps under their own policies. Support and privacy contact: satwik1330@gmail.com. This policy is for the Firebase edition and must not be used as the Local edition’s Play Data safety description.
