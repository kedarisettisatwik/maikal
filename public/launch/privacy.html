MANTRA Local privacy policy

About this policy

MANTRA Local is an on-device diary for goals, tasks, events, daily reflections, notes, manual expenses, alarms and screen-time review. Effective 4 November 2026. Contact: satwik1330@gmail.com.

Data kept on your device

Your entries, Note of the day text, tags, saved links, completion history, manual amounts, alarm schedules and preferences are stored in app-private storage. The Local APK has no Internet permission. It does not send diary records, identifiers, usage counts or analytics to MANTRA or Firebase. There is no sign-up, cloud account, advertising or advertising SDK in this edition.

Optional Usage access

If you enable screen-time tracking, Android Usage access allows MANTRA to read app usage and display time per app and daily totals. This is optional, stays on your device, and can be disabled in Android Settings > Special app access > Usage access. MANTRA can use the daily total for a screen-time goal only when you enable that option.

Notifications and ringing alarms

Optional notification permission shows reminders and tracker reply controls. Exact-alarm and full-screen special access support alarms you schedule. A foreground service plays the alarm sound and vibrates while ringing, with a visible notification and Snooze/Stop controls. Alarms stop automatically after ten minutes. Android may require explicit permission before full-screen alarms can appear. Permission denial does not prevent keeping diary records.

Sharing and external apps

When you share text or a link to MANTRA, it receives only the text/link you choose and saves it on this device. It does not read other apps’ notifications, messages, accounts, contacts or bank transactions. Opening a saved external link launches your browser or another app, whose privacy practices apply. MANTRA does not silently upload link content.

Retention, deletion and security

Entries remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Some deletion markers and calculated history may remain in app-private storage to keep records consistent; clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes the complete local diary. Android backup is disabled, so uninstalling or clearing storage loses your data. MANTRA does not maintain a server copy of Local data and cannot recover it. Android app isolation and device security protect storage; this is not a claim of separate end-to-end or application-level database encryption.

Children and sensitive content

MANTRA is a general productivity tool, not a service directed to children. Do not put passwords, payment-card credentials or other secrets into diary entries. No bank connection or automated financial advice is provided.

Contact and changes

Questions about privacy or support: satwik1330@gmail.com. We may update this policy when the app changes; the effective date and public policy page will be updated. The download website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary website connection information separately from the offline APK.
